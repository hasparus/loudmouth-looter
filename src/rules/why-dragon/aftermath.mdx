---
title: Aftermath
---

When _the Dragon or the Hunters are defeated_:

Take **1 XP** if you have defeated the Dragon.\
Take **1 XP** if you praise another Hunter's deed. No duplicates.

For each unspent **XP** you hold, choose one from the following:

- **Fulfill your Goal.** Describe how getting what you wanted changes your life.
- **Clear 1 Pyre**. Time and distance heal your wounds.
- **Claim a piece of the Dragon** that wasn't destroyed: a Scale, a Fang, a
  Curse.

Choices are repeatable. If you **Relinquished your Goal**, describe the life you
found in its place.

Then, go around the table once again.

If you're **consumed** and dead, name the legend told about you. If you're
**consumed**, but alive, tell of the desolation you've wrought upon the world.
Otherwise, for each marked **Pyre**, another Hunter describes a shadow over your
future.

The DM looks at the remaining pieces of the Dragon and narrates their future.
