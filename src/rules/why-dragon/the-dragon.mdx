---
title: The Dragon
---

The Dragon is made of Barriers: _Scales_ to shield it, _Fangs_ to deter
attackers, and _Curses_ to spell doom if it ever dies. Each is _Known_ or
_Secret_ until a Hunter unveils it, and each can be destroyed — or taken. Put
them in the center of the table. For short games, use 3–5 and start _in medias
res_; for four-hour games, add 1–2 more and a Curse on top.
