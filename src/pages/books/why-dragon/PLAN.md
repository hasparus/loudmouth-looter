# Why Would You Fight a Dragon?

## Object

One A4 sheet, folded once into four A5 pages. This is not a blog layout; it is the printable game.

The PDF plays three roles:

* front cover / invitation
* inside rules spread
* Hunter playbook / character sheet

Design target:

> Blades in the Dark information design + MÖRK BORG emotional force + medieval manuscript/woodcut surface.

# Core Visual Direction

Use:

* off-white paper
* black body text
* dark crimson for titles, danger, move names, Pyre, result numbers
* deep green for vines, ornaments, medieval structure
* medieval marginalia only as navigation/texture, not content

Avoid:

* fake parchment overkill
* equal-box spreadsheet layout
* modern UI cards
* decorative clutter
* every move having an illustration

The page should feel playable first, beautiful second.

---

# Typography Rules

## Fonts

Two faces, same as the website.

Avara Black — titles, move names, decorated initials, result numbers. The heavy display voice.

Crimson Text — body, triggers, all running prose. Regular for text, italic for triggers.

No Junicode, no third face. Both are already self-hosted (`public/fonts/Avara-Black.woff2`, `@fontsource/crimson-text`).

## Move headings

Each move begins with one decorated medieval initial.

The first letter must not appear twice.

Correct:

[H] ear Me Roar

Wrong:

[H] Hear Me Roar

The initial is small and inset. Text flows beside it and below it.

## Triggers

All move triggers are italic.

Example:

*When you chase your Goal with abandon...*

## Result numbers

All resolution numbers are bold.

Examples:

**10+**

**7–9**

**6−**

## Body text

Crimson Text, regular weight.

Do not sacrifice usability for gothic flavor.

## Hunter page content

Title:

Hunter

## Class

CLASS: ______________________

## Stats

No circles.

No pick-track.

Write-in values only.

BODY ______
WITS ______
SOUL ______

Instruction:

Distribute values from −2 to +2 so their total equals +1.

Examples:

+2, 0, −1
+1, +1, −1
+1, 0, 0
+2, +1, −2

Stat meanings:

Body — physical strength and stamina.
Wits — mental acuity and perception.
Soul — arcane potency and spiritual force.

## Goal

Checklist plus write-in.

☐ Glory
☐ Greed
☐ Knowledge
☐ Oath
☐ Power
☐ Vengeance
☐ ______________________

The blank line matters. It lets in Love, Hunger, Home, Freedom, whatever fits. The canonical Goals are examples, not a cage.

## XP

Four boxes.

XP: □ □ □ □

Rule:

When you have 4 XP or more and a moment of rest, spend 4 XP to raise one stat by +1, up to +3, or clear 1 Pyre.

# Pyre Design

Latest decision: the Pyre lives on the left rail of the Hunter playbook, not across the full width. Make it one big flame, and only one: not four boxes, not a row of little flames, not a full-width ruler. It still works as a paperclip gauge, but it reads first as a single doom-flame.

Possible solution:

A large vertical crimson flame on the left rail.

Next to it or inside it:

* Kindled / start
* three discreet tick marks
* Consumed

Instruction:

Slide a paperclip along the Pyre.

When it reaches Consumed, you are dead or worse.

The flame is the icon.
The paperclip track is the interface.
The whole thing should feel like a physical doom gauge.

# Information Architecture

Blades influence:

* Put the most-used information where the eye returns often.
* Make rules behave like an interface.
* Repeated syntax must be identical.
* Reduce prose.
* Group by use during play, but preserve the move order from the article.
* Hunter sheet should be functional, not decorative.

MÖRK BORG influence:

* One violent dominant visual.
* Bold asymmetry.
* Emotional contrast.
* Crimson as danger.
* Dramatic scale shifts.
* Do not make everything equally polite.

Medieval influence:

* rubricated headings
* decorated initials
* green vines as dividers
* manuscript ornaments
* woodcut dragon / battlefield
* no fake handwritten jokes
